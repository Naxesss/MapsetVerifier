JMdict and JMnedict (Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group)
Readings of Japanese words and names and spellings of loan words, included in the romanisation check as a word list
(MapsetVerifier.Checks/Utils/Romanisation/japanese-words.txt.br, generated by scripts/generate-japanese-words.mjs).
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
https://www.edrdg.org/enamdict/enamdict_doc.html
